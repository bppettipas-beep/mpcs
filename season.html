01OPEN QUALIFIERS · BO3

EARN A PLACE.

Any number of teams may register. The qualifier bracket is created after signups close, starting with sixteen teams by default. Eight teams advance.

02CHAMPIONSHIP ROUND OF 16 · BO3

THE FIELD UNITES.

Eight qualifier winners face eight invited teams. Each team bans two kits for its matchup, leaving three kits to play.

03QUARTERFINALS & SEMIFINALS · BO5

SURVIVE THE BRACKET.

Each team bans one kit for its matchup, leaving five kits in the series.

04GRAND FINAL · BO7

BECOME THE FIRST.

No kits are banned. The finalists compete across the complete seven-kit pool.
