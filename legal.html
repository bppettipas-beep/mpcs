By using this website or participating in MPCS activities, you agree to follow published MPCS rules, staff decisions, platform requirements, and applicable law. MPCS may update schedules, formats, rules, eligibility requirements, and event details when reasonably necessary.

PRIVACY

MPCS may collect information voluntarily provided through Discord or staff communications, including usernames, contact details, roster information, and competition records. It is used to operate events, communicate with participants, enforce rules, and publish results.

COMPETITION RULES

Official rules, kit settings, match procedures, conduct requirements, disputes, and penalties are published through the MPCS Discord. Cheating, harassment, match manipulation, account sharing, and attempts to evade enforcement may result in removal.
